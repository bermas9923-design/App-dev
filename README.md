## 🎬 Overview
**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series created by *The Duffer Brothers* and released on **Netflix**.  
It’s set in the 1980s in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, where strange supernatural events unfold after a young boy goes missing.

---

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – A loyal and brave friend  
- **Jim Hopper (David Harbour)** – The town’s sheriff  
- **Joyce Byers (Winona Ryder)** – Determined mother searching for her son  
- **Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo)** – The group’s science whiz  

---

## 🔮 What Makes It Great
1. 1980s nostalgia 🕹️  
2. Mysterious and suspenseful storyline  
3. Strong friendship and teamwork themes  
4. Fantastic soundtrack (especially *"Should I Stay or Should I Go"*) 🎵
